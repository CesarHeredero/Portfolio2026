## Context

Flexicar's vehicle appraisal process has 3 steps: enter license plate, see estimated valuation, and book an appointment. Data showed significant drop-off at each transition, especially between step 2 (see valuation) and step 3 (confirm appointment).

The team assumed the problem was price. But recorded sessions showed something different: users were leaving to find more information, not necessarily dissatisfied with the valuation.

## Problem

- **Single channel**: only phone was available to resolve doubts. Many users didn't want to call.
- **Channel friction**: switching from digital to phone breaks the flow.
- **Poorly defined objective**: the team measured "completed appraisals" (form submitted), not the real goal (in-store appointment).
- **No recovery scenarios**: if a user abandoned, there was no way to re-engage them.

## Action

### Discovery

- Recorded session analysis to identify abandonment patterns.
- Interviews with the store team to understand the real value of each lead type.
- KPI redefinition: from "form submitted" to "in-store appointment confirmed".

### WhatsApp scenario design

I defined 4 WhatsApp Business integration scenarios:

1. **At step 1** (enter license plate): Help CTA for users who don't know their exact plate.
2. **At step 2** (see valuation): Negotiation CTA for users who want to discuss the price.
3. **On abandonment** (exit intent): Recovery message with a direct link to where they left off.
4. **Post-form** (appointment confirmation): Confirmation message with store details.

### Coordination

- Technical specification for WhatsApp Business API integration.
- CRM team briefing to correctly tag leads by originating scenario.
- Template message design with the Communications team.
- MVP measurement criteria and iteration plan.

## Impact

The low-friction channel went live. In-store appointments increased in the segment that previously abandoned at step 2.

The KPI redesign had a valuable side effect: it aligned Marketing, UX and Store teams around the same objective, reducing noise in performance reviews.
