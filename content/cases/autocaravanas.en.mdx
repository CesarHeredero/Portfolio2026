## Context

Flexicar operated exclusively in the used car segment. The leisure vehicle market (motorhomes, campers, converted vans) showed sustained post-pandemic growth with higher margins and less direct digital competition.

The opportunity was clear: expand the marketplace without needing new physical store infrastructure, leveraging the existing platform.

## Problem

- **No catalogue**: there were no leisure vehicles in the inventory.
- **Unadapted UX**: search filters were designed for cars (horsepower, seats, doors). Motorhomes have completely different attributes (length, sleeping berths, tank capacity).
- **No stock provider**: we needed a motorhome data feed with national coverage.
- **SEO from scratch**: no authority in the sector, no URL architecture or sitemaps for the vertical.
- **Cannibalization risk**: expanding the catalogue without the right structure could dilute the main SEO.

## Action

### Phases 1-3: Discovery & feasibility

- Market research: competitor analysis, search volume by category, average price.
- Identification and evaluation of 3 potential motorhome feed providers.
- Technical validation: feed compatibility with existing data structure.
- MVP definition: minimum attributes a listing needs to be useful.

### Phases 4-6: Product & data architecture

- URL architecture design: `/motorhomes/[brand]/[model]/` with correct canonicals.
- New filter specification: type (integral, semi-integrated, overcab, camper van), length, sleeping berths.
- Extended data model for the provider feed.
- Independent sitemaps for the leisure vertical.

### Phases 7-9: Implementation & launch

- Dev coordination for feed integration and listing rendering.
- QA of the first 500 listings: images, prices, attributes, canonical URLs.
- Schema.org setup: `Vehicle` + `Offer` for each listing.
- Staged launch by brand and Search Console monitoring plan.

## Impact

New business line live on the marketplace, accessible in both Spain and Portugal. The motorhome vertical became an incremental organic traffic source with high-intent searches and lower SEO competition than the used car segment.
