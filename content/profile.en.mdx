## About me

Senior Product Owner with 6+ years in digital product and a solid background in UX, technical SEO and data. Currently at Flexicar, Spain and Portugal's largest used car marketplace, where I lead cross-functional projects across product, marketing, infrastructure and data.

My approach is technical without losing sight of strategy: I understand code well enough to spec correctly, I measure before deciding, and I coordinate teams without losing focus on the user.

## What I do

- **Product**: I define roadmaps, write user stories with precise acceptance criteria, facilitate refinements and prioritize with data.
- **UX**: I design flows, prototype in Figma and conduct research when the team lacks capacity.
- **Technical SEO**: sitemaps, Schema.org, Core Web Vitals, URL architecture, crawl budget management.
- **Data**: I spec tracking, coordinate with the Data team and build dashboards in Looker Studio.

## How I work

I prefer evidence-based decisions over opinion-based ones. When there's no data, I find a way to generate it before committing resources.

I operate well in ambiguous environments with multiple stakeholders. I know when to escalate and when to just move forward.

I work remotely from Madrid with availability for occasional on-site visits.
